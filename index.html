BACKGROUND & MOTIVATION

The Problem: Friction in a 600+ Acre Campus

Massive Campus Scale

With 600+ acres, freshers and visitors frequently get disoriented trying to find tech labs in Blocks 32 to 38, UniMall, or hospital facilities.

Scattered Hostel Mess Menus

Students across 14+ hostels have no real-time way to know the 4-meal rotation (Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner) or nutritional content.

Mess Queues & Timing Rush

Students arrive blindly during peak hours, resulting in long waiting queues, hall congestion, and missed meal windows.

Manual Mess Leave Rebates

Students on leaves (>3 days) face complex manual paperwork to claim their legitimate ₹130/day mess fee refunds.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

CampusGrid: An Integrated Campus Ecosystem

01

GIS Campus Map

Leaflet engine with GPS pins for all academic blocks, hospitals, gates, and hostels with instant fly-to search.

02

Live Mess Portal

7-Day cyclical timetable with live countdown timer detecting current active meals and closing times.

03

Digital Verto ID

Student authentication with registration verification, hostel allocation, and digital campus card modal.

04

Warden & Rebates

Automated rebate refund calculator (₹130/day) with Warden review console and broadcast alert marquee.

TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

System Architecture & Tech Stack

Presentation Layer (Frontend UI)

Tailwind CSS (Aesthetic Cyber Dark Theme), Lucide Icons, Glassmorphism, and responsive mobile bottom-bar.

HTML5 + Tailwind

Geospatial Mapping Engine

Leaflet.js v1.9.4 with OpenStreetMap tiles, custom neon DivIcons, and camera fly-to animations.

Leaflet.js + OSM

State Management & Persistence

Client-side auth session, persistent dish voting, community review feed, and rebate queue in LocalStorage.

LocalStorage API

Mobile PWA Architecture

Service Worker (sw.js) for asset caching and manifest.json for 1-click home screen install on Android & iOS.

Progressive Web App
